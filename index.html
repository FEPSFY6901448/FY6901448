Conference paper:DNA data storage

Abstract

  The explosion of digital data in volume urgently requires the finding of a sustainable and durable method of storage. In nature, DNA is the ultimate data archive that holds immense promise for compact, high-density, and long-term preservation. The essay highlights how DNA-based data storage has the potential to change environmental conservation with the secure preservation of biodiversity records and ecological information. It considers recent developments, challenges, and the place of the technology in securing important knowledge for future generations. DNA outcompetes traditional storage systems in longevity, sustainability, and efficiency; hence, it is an innovative solution to both data and conservation crises.
